## it
# Cambio lingua
Con uno sguardo ai codici ISO di [lingua](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_ISO_639_language_codes) e [paese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ISO_3166-1_alpha-2), per esempio en-US ed en-GB, [uk-UA](https://www.ukrainer.net/) e [ru-UA](https://www.ukrainer.net/ru/), [lld-IT](https://home.provinzia.bz.it/lld/home) e [it-IT](https://home.provincia.bz.it/it/home).

## en
# Language switch
With a look at ISO [language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_ISO_639_language_codes) and [country](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ISO_3166-1_alpha-2) codes, e.g. en-US and en-GB, [uk-UA](https://www.ukrainer.net/) and [ru-UA](https://www.ukrainer.net/ru/), [lld-IT](https://home.provinzia.bz.it/lld/home) and [it-IT](https://home.provincia.bz.it/it/home).

## fr
